Option 02 · Singapore gateway

KIX→SIN→PBH→BUT→PBH→SIN→KIX

SIN is possible, but date-sensitive

The Singapore route can work, but it needs an extra Singapore night or a later Bhutan arrival. It is not a clean swap for the current 23 November Paro arrival.

Singapore changes the return date: Tuesday 1 December does not match the current Paro → Singapore operating pattern. The likely alternatives are leaving on Monday 30 November or Wednesday 2 December; the SIN → KIX flight then follows.

Before we price

Three things the timetable must respect.

  1. Start with KIX. Include Kameoka → KIX transfer time and the first international flight.
  2. Protect 26 November. The birthday should not depend on a long road transfer or a late gateway connection.
  3. Leave a real buffer. Separate KIX and Bhutan tickets need an overnight or a generous connection; optimism is not baggage insurance.
