Before we price

Three things the timetable must respect.

  1. Start with KIX. Include Kameoka → KIX transfer time and the first international flight.
  2. Protect 26 November. The birthday should not depend on a long road transfer or a late gateway connection.
  3. Leave a real buffer. Separate KIX and Bhutan tickets need an overnight or a generous connection; optimism is not baggage insurance.
