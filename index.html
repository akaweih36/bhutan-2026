The question

How much Bhutan comes after the festival?

Stay with Bumthang

Make Central Bhutan the trip: festival, monasteries, craft and slower days around Bumthang. Less geographical variety, more room for the reason we are going.

Add the west

After Bumthang, continue to Phobjikha, Punakha and Paro. More landscape and food; more road and more hotel arithmetic.

The non-negotiable

26 November stays in Bumthang. It should not become a scenic transfer with a cake waiting at the other end.

Choose one or two routes above, then send the same brief to suitable operators. Ask whether they can customise the route, keep 26 November free from a long transfer, and quote it for the actual group size. Compare what they can genuinely deliver; this page will not invent a total.

The latest proposal includes in its stated total: tour component, GST, SDF and visa fees. The detailed inclusions for accommodation, meals, guide, ground transport and entrance fees should be reconfirmed in the revised quotation.

Detailed nine-day proposal

Excursion to Himalayas · Company #1

Operator proposal: 23 November–1 December, with Thimphu on the 24th, a Paro → Bumthang flight on the 25th, a festival and birthday day on the 26th, then Bumthang → Paro by flight and a 4–5 hour road transfer to Punakha on the 27th. Phobjikha follows on the 28th, Paro on the 29th and Tiger’s Nest on the 30th.

Our reading: The structure is workable, but the document calls 26 November “Jambay Lhakhang Drup Festival”. The official tentative 2026 calendar instead lists Jambay Lhakhang Drup on 26–29 October, Jambay Lhakhang Singye Cham on 24 November and Nalakhar Tshechu on 24–26 November. We would keep the birthday in Bumthang, ask the operator to name the exact event, and make the 27th flight-and-road timing explicit before sending it back for revision. The full day-by-day detail remains in the read-only planning document.
